### Standee Technical Setup (For the Designer)

This document defines the **exact technical and visual setup** for the JUNO FYP standee so the designer can produce a print-ready PSD that matches both **university requirements** and **project branding**.

---

## 1. Technical Specifications

- **Dimensions**: 24" width × 60" height (**portrait** orientation)
- **Resolution**: **330 DPI**
- **File Type**: **PSD** (Adobe Photoshop source file)
- **Design Tools**:
  - Use **Adobe Photoshop** or **Adobe Illustrator**
  - **Strictly no Canva**
- **Color Palette**:
  - **Deep Navy**: `#0B0D17` (primary background)
  - **Electric Indigo**: `#5842FF` (primary accent)
  - **Neon Purple**: `#A230FF` (secondary accent / highlight)

Use gradients and glassmorphism effects based on these three core colors.

---

## 2. Section 1 – The Academic Header (Mandatory)

This is the **top band** of the standee and must respect academic branding rules.

- **Top Left**:
  - **FAST-NUCES Logo** (clean version, **no Ayah**)
  - Ensure the logo is high-resolution and not stretched.

- **Top Center**:
  - **Project Title (Largest Font)**:
    - Text: **JUNO**
    - This should be the **most prominent text** on the header.

- **Top Right**:
  - **Supervisor**:
    - Format: `Supervisor: Dr./Mr./Engr. [Full Name]`
  - **Team** (directly below supervisor line):
    - Format:  
      `Team: [Name 1 – Roll No], [Name 2 – Roll No], ...`

- **Main Headline (Below Header Bar)**:
  - Place this just under the header strip, centered across the standee:
  - Text:  
    **"The Pulse of Global Commerce: AI-Powered Shopify Communication"**

Use a slightly smaller size than the JUNO title, but still bold and easily readable from ~5 feet.

---

## 3. Section 2 – Problem vs. Solution (Chaos → Control Flow)

This is the **hero visual area** of the standee. Divide it into **two vertical or diagonally slanted panes** to show the transition from chaos to control.

### Left Side – “The Communication Gap” (The Chaos)

- **Label**:
  - **"The Communication Gap"**
- **Visual Style**:
  - Darker background using **Deep Navy** with red/orange accents.
  - Use **tangled lines** or messy connectors between bubbles to show disorganization.
- **Notification Bubbles (examples)**:
  - “Where is my order?”
  - “Wrong Address!”
  - “Supplier hasn’t replied.”
- **Supporting Text (Bullets)**:
  - **Fragmented Channels**
  - **Slow Response Times**

Keep text minimal; this side should visually feel **stressful and cluttered**.

### Right Side – “The JUNO Standard” (The Control)

- **Label**:
  - **"The JUNO Standard"**
- **Visual Style**:
  - Clean, bright **indigo gradient** background (Electric Indigo + Neon Purple).
  - Use **glassmorphism**: translucent panels with blur and subtle borders.
- **Dashboard Mockup (Key Highlights)**:
  - **Unified Inbox**:
    - Visual: single clean inbox pane.
    - Caption: *“Orders linked to messages.”*
  - **Smart Labels**:
    - Example label: *“Refund Request – Angry”* with an obvious “AI tag / chip”.
    - Emphasize AI-detected **intent and mood**.
  - **JUNOCHAT Panel**:
    - Show structured supplier instructions:
      - Order ID
      - Item(s)
      - Clear requested action (e.g., “Reship”)
      - Priority / deadline
    - Caption: *“Structured, unambiguous supplier instructions.”*

This side should feel **calm, controlled, and futuristic**, clearly opposite to the left side.

---

## 4. Section 3 – Core Value Pillars (“How It Works”)

Use the **three-column card layout** from your existing UI/theme. Each column is a **card** with a small icon, title, and one-line description.

| **For Vendors**              | **The AI Hub**             | **For Suppliers**                    |
|------------------------------|----------------------------|--------------------------------------|
| **Automated Fulfillment**    | **Intent Intelligence**    | **Structured Coordination**          |
| Real-time sync of Shopify orders to suppliers instantly. | AI detects mood and intent to prevent chargebacks. | No more vague emails; just clear, actionable data. |

Design notes:
- Cards should use **glassmorphic panels** with Electric Indigo / Neon Purple highlights.
- Icons should be simple and tech-forward (e.g., truck/order icon, brain/AI icon, network/flow icon).

---

## 5. Section 4 – Tech Stack & Onboarding

This is a **smaller informational band**, likely below the value pillars.

### 5.1 Technologies Used

Use **small, clean icons** beside short labels. Suggested entries:

- **Shopify API**
- **OpenAI / LLMs**
- **React / Node.js**
- **Webhooks**

Keep this to one line or a compact grid—avoid long descriptions.

### 5.2 Onboarding Journey (Horizontal Timeline)

Show a **3-step horizontal timeline** with minimal text:

1. **Connect** – Secure Shopify API Sync.  
2. **Configure** – Set Sync & Fulfillment Preferences.  
3. **Automate** – AI-driven workflows go live.

Visually, each step can be a small node with a short label and icon.

---

## 6. Section 5 – Footer & Engagement

This is the **bottom band** of the standee, driving interaction.

- **Main Tagline**:
  - Text:  
    **"Everything you need to dominate the market"**
  - Centered or slightly left-aligned above the QR and buttons.

- **QR Code**:
  - Place a **large, branded QR code** in the **bottom right corner**.
  - Label under/next to it:
    - **"Scan for Live Demo/Project Details"**

- **Role Call-to-Action (Mock Buttons)**:
  - Use **button-style graphics** (not real buttons) to show the two sides of the platform:
    - **Sign Up as Vendor**
    - **Sign Up as Supplier**
  - Place these near the QR code or centered above it, clearly indicating it is a **two-sided marketplace**.

---

## 7. Important Instructions for the Designer

- **Readability**:
  - Ensure the **project title** (`JUNO`) and **core value pillars** are clearly readable from at least **5 feet away**.
  - Avoid overly thin fonts for key headings.

- **Visuals Over Text**:
  - Prioritize **dashboard screen captures**, **AI icons**, and **simple diagrams** over paragraphs.
  - All descriptions should be **1–2 short lines** only.

- **Consistency & Branding**:
  - Use the specified **indigo gradient** and **glassmorphism** (transparency + blur + soft borders) to match app screenshots and maintain brand identity.
  - Keep spacing, corner radii, and typography style consistent across all cards and panels.

The goal is a standee that is **instantly scannable, visually premium, and clearly communicates JUNO’s value** to both vendors and suppliers.
