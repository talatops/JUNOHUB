### Standee Concept: “Chaos to Control”

This concept is designed to be **high-impact** and instantly relatable to both **Vendor** and **Supplier** personas, visually contrasting the “old way” of dropshipping operations with **The JUNO Way**.

---

## Top Header

- **Main Headline**:  
  **SCALE YOUR REACH. SIMPLIFY YOUR SUPPLY.**

Position this prominently at the top of the standee, above the split-screen section, with strong, bold typography.

---

## Split-Screen Comparison (Problem vs Solution)

Use a **left/right split** to immediately show “before vs after”.

### Left Side – The Problem (“The Old Way”)

- **Visual Style**:
  - Darker, slightly noisy / cluttered background.
  - Red or orange accents to convey stress and urgency.
- **Notification Bubbles** (examples):
  - “Where is my order?”
  - “Wrong Address!”
  - “Supplier hasn’t replied.”
- **Label / Tagline**:
  - **Fragmented & Slow**
- **Overall Feel**:
  - Chaotic, multiple channels, unclear ownership, lots of manual work.

### Right Side – The Solution (“The JUNO Way”)

- **Visual Style**:
  - Bright, clean, glowing **indigo** background.
  - Futuristic but minimal UI elements.
- **Dashboard Mockup (Key Elements)**:
  - **Unified Inbox**:
    - Messages and orders are visually connected.
    - Example annotation: *“Orders linked to messages automatically.”*
  - **Smart Labels**:
    - Tag examples:  
      “Refund Request – Angry – High Priority”
    - Emphasize AI-driven mood + intent detection.
  - **JUNOCHAT Panel**:
    - Show a clear, structured reshipment request:
      - Order reference
      - Items
      - Action: “Reship”
      - Deadline / priority
    - Callout text: *“Clear, structured reshipment requests.”*
- **Label / Tagline**:
  - **Connected & Controlled**

---

## Middle Section – Core Value Pillars

Use a **three-column card layout**, consistent with your existing UI/theme.

Each card should have:
- An icon or small illustration.
- A short title.
- 1–2 lines of supporting text.

### Card 1 – For Vendors

- **Title**: **Automated Fulfillment**
- **Subtitle / Description**:
  - “Sync Shopify orders to suppliers instantly.”
  - Emphasize reduced manual work and faster response time.

### Card 2 – The AI Hub

- **Title**: **Intent Intelligence**
- **Subtitle / Description**:
  - “AI detects mood and promises to prevent chargebacks.”
  - Highlight:
    - Intent detection
    - Sentiment/mood analysis
    - Policy-aware responses that reduce disputes

### Card 3 – For Suppliers

- **Title**: **Structured Coordination**
- **Subtitle / Description**:
  - “No more vague emails; just actionable data.”
  - Emphasize:
    - Clear order references
    - Standardized requests
    - Less back-and-forth

---

## Footer Area

The footer should reinforce the overall promise and invite action.

### Tagline

- **Text**:  
  **Everything you need to dominate the market**

Place this as a bold line above the QR and buttons.

### QR Code

- Large, branded **QR code** in one bottom corner (e.g., bottom-right).
- Label near the QR, e.g.:
  - “Scan to book a demo”
  - or “Scan to explore JUNO”

### Call-to-Action Buttons (Visual Only)

Use button-style graphics to show that JUNO is a **two-sided marketplace**:

- **Button 1**:  
  **Sign Up as Vendor**
- **Button 2**:  
  **Sign Up as Supplier**

These are **mock-up UI buttons** on the standee (not functional), but they visually communicate:
- There are dedicated paths for both vendors and suppliers.
- JUNO serves the entire chain from **customer → vendor → supplier**.

---

## Overall Design Notes

- Keep text **short and punchy**; the standee should be scannable in a few seconds.
- Use your **brand colors** (especially indigo and accent colors) to tie the standee back to the product UI.
- Lean on **icons, highlights, and short captions** rather than long paragraphs.
- Ensure the **“Chaos vs Control”** contrast is immediately visible from a distance.
